    Architecture, engineering evidence and product direction

    The asset. A FastAPI evidence layer that ingests FHIR R4 and mapped CSV, renders DICOM or JPEG pixels, and ties events to one encounter. It distinguishes when a measurement was recorded from when the result became available. Clinicians can inspect the original source and flag missing or uncertain information.

    120 labs · 127 vitals · 1 linked chest X-rayReported in a restricted-local acceptance run. The runnable synthetic six-hour review separately demonstrates saved reviews and auditable corrections.
